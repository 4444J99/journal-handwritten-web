# SOP: Using Meta Muse with Instagram for the "n/100" Handwritten Poetry Series (verified against primary sources 2026-11-03)

Muse can work for this project only as a read-only, advise-only assistant: connect it to the new-identity Instagram account alone, keep every permission on "Always ask," never give it scans, drafts or files, and use it for reminders, checklists and one Week-12 analytics summary. Meta's own terms make Anthony "solely responsible" for anything Muse does, and Meta warns that Muse's outputs "may be inaccurate," so the agent should not be trusted to post, write, reply or keep his data private on its own.\[1\]

## TL;DR

- **What Muse is (PRIMARY):** Meta's personal AI agent, launched September 8, 2026. It runs in a per-user "Muse Secure VM" with a separate "Sentinel" agent that has to approve every action and all network traffic.\[2\]\[3\] It is 18+ only.\[4\] It launched in the US and has since been announced for Canada (Sept 29), Japan (Oct 23, per press citing Meta Japan's newsroom; reported as its first market outside North America) and Singapore (Oct 27). Facebook, Instagram and Threads accounts in the same Accounts Center connect to it automatically.\[5\] Meta's line on actions is that "nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your approval." Muse Confidential VM, with user-held encryption keys, was promised for "later this year" and is not documented as launched.\[2\]
- **What Muse may touch:** Insights-level account data for the new identity only, plus reminders and checklists. It must not get scans, drafts, Reserve poems, private journal pages, DMs, email, cloud drives or Mac file access, and it must never draft captions, notes or replies. Two settings carry most of the safety. Under Settings → Permissions, choose "Always ask." Under Settings → Data controls, turn off "Help improve our AI models," which is on by default. Keep personal and ETCETER4 accounts out of the Accounts Center that Muse uses, because Meta can disable "associated Meta accounts" for Muse violations.
- **Posting mechanics (PRIMARY Instagram Help/@creators):** Use up to 5 hashtags.\[6\] Upload at 1080 px wide in aspect ratios between 1.91:1 and 3:4; 1080×1440 is the tallest that is kept uncropped.\[7\] Write your own alt text under Advanced Settings.\[8\] Account-level Insights only look back 90 days, so the Week-12 review has to happen by day 84 after poem 1.\[9\] A carousel cannot be partly edited after posting; the only fix is deleting the whole post.\[10\] Register every poem through GRUW before posting, because showing it online may or may not count as "publication" in the copyright sense, while journals are explicit: Poetry magazine's submission page says "Work that has appeared online for any reason, including social media posts, is considered previously published."

## Part B — Standard Operating Procedure

**SOP ID:** N100-MUSE-IG-01 · **Owner and sole decision-maker:** Anthony · **Time zone:** America/New_York · **Last verified against primary sources:** 2026-11-03

### B0. One-Page Weekly Checklist (print this)

**Gate checks (every item must be YES, or do not post):**
1. [ ] The poem is NOT on the Reserve list. Check it against the offline Reserve list itself, not from memory.
2. [ ] The poem is in a GRUW batch whose application was submitted, with the confirmation number recorded in the release log, BEFORE this post.
3. [ ] The page is not flagged private/sensitive. Margins and the backs of pages are checked for other text or names.
4. [ ] The scan is unaltered. No AI edits, "aging," upscaling or generative fill. Only crop, rotate and exposure adjustments made by Anthony.
5. [ ] Every word in the caption, end note and alt text was written by Anthony. Nothing was pasted from Muse or any AI.
6. [ ] The account shown in the Instagram app is the new-identity handle, not personal or ETCETER4.

**Format checks:**
7. [ ] Image exported at 1080 px wide, aspect between 1.91:1 and 3:4.\[7\] Default is 1080×1440 (3:4) for tall journal pages.
8. [ ] Zoom to 100% on a phone: every line of handwriting is legible. If not, split the page across carousel frames.
9. [ ] Carousel frames are in final order. They cannot be reordered after posting.\[10\]
10. [ ] "n/100" number is correct and matches the release log.
11. [ ] Hashtags: 0–5, chosen from the pre-approved list.\[6\] Typos checked.
12. [ ] Alt text written by Anthony and entered under Advanced Settings → Write Alt Text, replacing the automatic alt text.\[8\]
13. [ ] Post time matches the fixed weekly slot.

**After posting:**
14. [ ] Open the live post once to confirm the image, caption, number and alt text.
15. [ ] Log the date, number, format and GRUW batch in the release log.
16. [ ] Do NOT open Insights. Do NOT ask Muse how the post is doing.
17. [ ] Replies and comments: answer only during the fixed weekly reply window, in your own words.

**Weeks 1–11:** no analytics. **Week 12 (by day 84 after poem 1):** run Procedure B8.

### B1. Purpose
Set out how Anthony runs a weekly release of handwritten poems on Instagram, and how Meta's Muse agent may be used for scheduling reminders, checklists and one analytics summary without any AI-written creative or interpretive text appearing anywhere and without exposing Reserve or private material.

### B2. Scope and Out-of-Scope
**In scope:** the new-identity Instagram account; weekly posting of the n/100 series; the GRUW and Reserve gates; Muse used for reminders, checklists, tag/format diagnostics and the Week-12 summary.

**Out of scope:**
- The ETCETER4 account and any personal account.
- TikTok and YouTube, until a later revision of this SOP.
- The interactive web piece.
- Literary journal submissions, except as a constraint.
- Legal advice. Section B12 is informational only.

### B3. Roles and Authority
3.1 **Anthony** decides everything: what to post, when, in what format, all text, all replies, and every interpretation of results.
3.2 **Muse and any other AI** may only advise on technical and logistical matters. It may not post, schedule, publish, comment, reply, DM, like, follow, edit, archive, delete, or change any Instagram setting. It may not write or suggest wording for any caption, note, footnote, alt text, reply or poem. It may not generate, alter or "enhance" any image, and may never generate images of Anthony's face.
3.3 Legal basis for keeping control: under the Muse Terms, the user is "solely responsible for the actions Muse takes or directs." Users must "maintain meaningful human oversight" and should not take "state-changing actions, such as sending communications… without appropriate human review and approval." Meta may respond to violations by "suspending or disabling your access to Muse and/or any associated Meta accounts." (PRIMARY, source 3)\[1\]

### B4. Prerequisites (complete BEFORE connecting Muse)
4.1 **Identity:** choose the name, handle and domain (Open Item 1). Create the Instagram account with an email address that is not used by the personal or ETCETER4 accounts.
4.2 **Account type:** set the account to public and switch it to a **Professional → Creator** account. Basis: Insights require a public account (PRIMARY, source 20),\[9\] and Meta describes Muse's analytics connector as covering "Instagram professional account analytics" (SECONDARY, sources 13–14).\[14\]\[38\] Requiring Creator rather than personal is an inference from those two facts.
4.3 **Accounts Center isolation:** Muse auto-connects Facebook, Instagram and Threads accounts that share an Accounts Center (PRIMARY, source 4).\[5\]\[39\] The Muse Terms say: "You can keep Muse in a separate Accounts Center by signing up with an email address that is not associated with another Meta account" (PRIMARY, source 3).\[1\] Pick one of these:
   - **Option A (recommended):** the new-identity Instagram account sits in its own Accounts Center with no personal or ETCETER4 profiles, and Muse is signed in through that Accounts Center.
   - **Option B:** Muse stays in a separate Accounts Center with no Instagram linked. Instagram figures are then entered manually at Week 12. This gives the most privacy and the least automation.
   - Do not sign into Muse with a Meta account whose Accounts Center contains the personal or ETCETER4 profiles.
   - Unverified: whether Muse can be limited to one of several Instagram accounts in a single Accounts Center. No primary documentation was found.
4.4 **Reserve gate:** keep the Reserve list (15–30 poems) offline only, on paper or a local file outside any synced cloud folder. Reserve poems are never scanned onto any device that Muse, Meta apps or cloud backup can reach.
4.5 **GRUW gate:** register each batch of up to 10 series poems through GRUW before any of them is posted. Every work in the batch must be unpublished, by the same author and claimant, in the same class, and uploaded as a separate file per work (PRIMARY, source 32).\[34\]\[35\]\[36\]
4.6 **Private-page gate:** keep an offline list of flagged journal pages. Those pages are never photographed with a phone that backs up to cloud services used by Muse.
4.7 **Device hygiene:** do NOT install the Muse Mac app, or do not grant it Full Disk Access, which "covers all the files on your Mac" (PRIMARY, source 9).\[40\] Use Muse on the phone or the web only.

### B5. One-Time Muse Setup
5.1 Sign in according to option 4.3 A or B.
5.2 **Settings → Data controls → turn OFF "Help improve our AI models" → Turn off.** This setting is on at first use, and changing it also applies to earlier interactions (PRIMARY, source 7).\[41\]
5.3 **Settings → Permissions:** set actions to **"Always ask"** so Muse asks "before any action." Set websites to "Always ask" (PRIMARY, source 5).\[42\]\[43\] When a prompt appears, choose only **"Allow once"** for reads, and **Deny** every write action that touches Instagram. Never choose "Always allow."
5.4 **Settings → Connectors:**
   - **Grant:** Instagram for the new identity only. This is read access for analytics.
   - **Withhold or disconnect:** Instagram Messages (if it appears), Facebook, Threads, Gmail or other email, Google Drive, Dropbox, Box, Notion, Calendar (optional, see 5.6), Canva, Figma, and every custom connector.
   - Disconnecting does not erase what Muse already learned, so ask it to "forget" afterwards (PRIMARY, source 4).\[39\]
5.5 **Standing instruction:** paste the block below into Muse and save it to Memory (Assistant icon → Identity → Memory). The text is technical only.
   > "Standing rules for the n/100 project: (1) You may not post, schedule, publish, edit, archive, delete, comment, reply, DM, like, follow, or change any Instagram setting. Propose no such actions. (2) You may not write or suggest any wording for captions, notes, alt text, replies or poems, and may not comment on the poems' meaning or quality. (3) You may not create or alter images. (4) Do not report, mention or summarize any Instagram metric, follower count or response before the Week-12 review date [DATE]. If asked earlier, reply only: 'Analytics locked until [DATE].' (5) Allowed: the weekly reminder, reading the pre-post checklist back to me, checking a hashtag list against a 5-tag limit and for typos, confirming the export dimensions I state, and the Week-12 report in the fixed format I provide."
5.6 **Reminders:** ask Muse for one weekly reminder at the fixed slot, e.g. "Every [weekday] [time] ET: 'n/100 release day — run checklist B0.'" Muse supports reminders and scheduled tasks that can be edited or deleted in chat (PRIMARY, source 7).\[44\] If you prefer not to depend on Muse, a phone calendar alarm does the same job.
5.7 **Limits on rule 4 (withholding):** no primary documentation says Muse can enforce a data embargo. It is an AI following an instruction, and Meta warns that outputs "may be inaccurate" (PRIMARY, source 3).\[1\] So the embargo is enforced by Anthony's own behavior: no opening Insights and no asking. Muse's rule is a backstop only. Also hide Muse's feed of FB/IG updates if it shows account activity (SECONDARY, source 12).\[13\]
5.8 **Do not use Muse share links.** Security firm Claroc's "GhostShares" research (October 18, 2026, reported by The Next Web and Quartz) found more than 6,800 Muse share hostnames with a single passive-DNS query, some exposing pages such as a loan application; per press timelines Meta paused sharing on October 20 and restored it on October 22, and Meta's statement said the flaw affected "shared artifacts," not chat sharing (SECONDARY).

### B6. Weekly Release Procedure
6.1 **T–7 days or earlier:** select the poem. Run gates 1–4 of B0. Confirm its GRUW batch has been filed.
6.2 **Scan or prepare the image yourself.** Use a flatbed or phone scan at high resolution, then export a copy at 1080 px width. Instagram resizes anything larger "down to a width of 1080 pixels" and crops ratios outside 1.91:1–3:4 (PRIMARY, source 18).\[7\] For handwriting:
   - Use 3:4 (1080×1440) as the default.
   - If the lines are small, split one page across 2–3 carousel frames, each at 3:4.
   - Make no AI enhancement or upscaling.
6.3 **Write the caption, end note and alt text by hand.** Use the fixed caption template set out in Open Item 3: number, title, date of the journal entry. Choose hashtags from the pre-approved list of 0–5 (PRIMARY, source 24).\[6\] Hashtags help classify posts but are not a reach lever (SECONDARY, source 25).\[26\]\[45\]
6.4 **Optional Muse diagnostic.** Paste only the hashtag list, never the poem or caption. Ask: "Count these and flag duplicates or typos." Ignore any suggested wording.
6.5 **Post manually in the Instagram app.** Go to Advanced Settings → Write Alt Text and enter your alt text for each frame, replacing the automatic alt text (PRIMARY, source 19).\[8\]
6.6 Complete steps 14–17 of B0.

### B7. "No Analytics Until 12 Poems" Rule
7.1 From poem 1 until the Week-12 review, Anthony does not open Insights, does not ask Muse about performance, and does not check follower counts.
7.2 **Timing constraint (PRIMARY, source 20):** account-level Insights cover only "the past 90 days."\[9\]\[46\]\[47\] If poem 1 posts on day 0, poem 12 posts around day 77. Hold the Week-12 review between **day 80 and day 85**, so that poem 1 is still inside the window and poem 12 has at least 3 days of data. Looking back further than 90 days needs a Meta One subscription (PRIMARY, source 23).\[25\]
7.3 **Measures fixed in advance (set exact thresholds in Open Item 4 before poem 1):**
   - **Distribution:** Reach/Viewers per post, and Reach from non-followers. Definition: "unique accounts that have seen this post… at least once and do not follow the account" (PRIMARY, source 22). Reach is estimated.\[24\]
   - **Response rate:** (saves + shares + comments + follows from post) ÷ reach.
   - **Intensity signals:** saves, shares, substantive comments, DMs, and follows attributed to a post (Follows definition, PRIMARY, source 21).\[23\]
   - Definitions of Saves and Shares were not found in a primary source. Use whatever the in-app (i) icon shows.

### B8. Week-12 Review Procedure
8.1 Schedule a single session. Either Anthony or Muse (read-only) pulls per-post figures into the fixed table: post #, format, date, reach, % non-follower reach, saves, shares, comments, follows. Muse may fill in the table only. It may not add commentary.
   - If Muse cannot read per-post reach or saves (a third party reports this limitation; UNVERIFIED), Anthony copies the figures from each post's "View insights" screen.\[5\]
8.2 **Sort each post into one of three cases using the thresholds set in advance:**

| Case | Pattern | Meaning | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Distribution | Reach below the threshold | Few people saw it; the data says nothing about the poem | Keep posting. Check that the slot time and hashtag list are fixed. Run one format test (B9) aimed at reach, e.g. a Reel version. Do not judge the poem |
| 2. Packaging/format | Reach at or above the threshold, response rate below the threshold | People saw it but did not engage | Run a format test (B9) on the same poem: carousel split vs single image, crop and legibility, caption length. Check legibility at 100% zoom |
| 3. Resonance | Reach low, but intensity signals present (saves, shares, a substantive comment or DM) | A small audience responded strongly | Note which posts. Keep the format. Consider a later re-release in a different format to widen reach |

8.3 Write the results in the revision log (B15). Do not look at Insights again until the next scheduled review, which is every 12 poems.

### B9. Format-Test Procedure
9.1 Test the format, never the poem. Pick one poem from Case 1 or 2.
9.2 Change exactly one variable, e.g. single image → 3-frame carousel, or carousel → Reel made of the unaltered scans with no AI motion. Keep the text, time slot and tags the same.
9.3 The re-release needs Anthony's own caption, marked as a re-release under the same n/100 number. Never delete the original just to repost it, unless the mistake protocol applies.
9.4 Read the result only at the next 12-poem review.

### B10. Replies and Comments
10.1 Anthony writes every reply, comment and DM himself, during a fixed weekly window.
10.2 Muse has no write permission and no Instagram Messages connector, so it does not read or collect DMs. If Anthony wants comments collected, Muse may list the comment text from the account's own posts in one batch at the Week-12 review. It may not draft or suggest any response.
10.3 Never paste a fan's DM or another person's personal information into Muse (Muse Terms §7.2 consent obligations; PRIMARY, source 3).\[1\]

### B11. Mistake Protocol (post went out with an error)
11.1 **Wrong tag, typo, or wrong caption or number:** use ⋯ → Edit to fix the caption or tags. The word "Edited" may appear (PRIMARY, source 28).\[10\] Log the fix. No other action.
11.2 **Wrong or missing alt text:** use ⋯ → Edit → Edit Alt Text (PRIMARY, source 19).\[8\]
11.3 **Wrong image order, wrong frame, or illegible frame in a carousel:** individual frames cannot be reordered, edited or deleted (PRIMARY, source 28).\[10\] Delete the post and repost the corrected version the same day under the same number. Log both.
11.4 **A Reserve poem, private page, or poem not yet registered was posted:** delete immediately; do not archive, because archiving only hides the post (PRIMARY, source 28).\[30\] Then:
   - Write down the time the post was live.
   - Move that poem out of journal eligibility, or register it as published if it was not yet registered (Open Item 6).
   - Deletion does not undo screenshots, shares, or copies already held under Meta's license.\[48\]
11.5 **Feelings rule:** a corrected mistake is logged as a technical event only. No checking of analytics is triggered.

### B12. Copyright and Publication (informational, not legal advice)
12.1 GRUW covers only works that are unpublished when the application is filed. "All works must be unpublished" (PRIMARY, source 32).\[34\]
12.2 The Copyright Office's practice manual treats a work as published online when the owner authorizes end users to keep copies, e.g. by download. It also states that a public "display does not of itself constitute publication" (PRIMARY, source 33).\[37\] On that reasoning, display-only posting on Instagram may not count as copyright "publication." The question has not been settled for Instagram specifically, because Instagram's Terms give Meta a broad, sub-licensable license (secondary wording, source 31).\[49\]\[50\] For that reason the SOP requires filing GRUW before posting in every case.
12.3 Literary journals use a stricter, contract-based meaning of "published": Poetry magazine's submission page states "Work that has appeared online for any reason, including social media posts, is considered previously published and should not be submitted," and Poetry Northwest excludes work published "on personal blogs and social media." Treat every series poem as permanently ineligible for those journals once it is posted. That is the reason the Reserve must never appear online.
12.4 Instagram's Terms of Use are being updated effective **2027-01-20** (PRIMARY snippet, source 31).\[51\] Re-read the license clause before that date (Open Item 7).

### B13. Data and Privacy Rules (what Muse may and may not see)
**May see:** Insights and metadata for the new-identity account only; the hashtag list; export dimensions Anthony types in; the checklist; dates.

**May never see:** poem text; scans or photos of any page; Reserve titles; draft captions or notes; the private-page list; DMs; personal or ETCETER4 accounts; email, Drive or Mac files.

**Why this list is strict:**
- Uploaded files are saved to the Library, and Muse "remembers files" (PRIMARY, source 8).\[52\]
- The Muse content license covers "Your Content… including from Connected Services" in order to provide and improve Muse (PRIMARY, source 3).\[1\]
- Meta may "monitor, log, review" Muse's actions (PRIMARY, source 3).\[1\]
- "Forget" removes data only "to the best of its ability" (PRIMARY, source 6).\[53\]
- Meta says Muse conversations and VM data are not shared with ad systems (PRIMARY, source 1).\[2\]
- Confidential VM, under which "not even Meta can access" the data, is not yet documented as live (PRIMARY, sources 1–2).\[2\]\[11\]

**Out of scope for Muse (Instagram side):** this research did not verify whether public Instagram posts are used for Meta AI training, or what opt-out exists (Open Item 8). The Muse training toggle covers Muse interactions only.\[41\]

**AI labels:** the scans are unaltered, so no "AI info" disclosure applies. Meta requires disclosure for photorealistic video or realistic audio that was digitally created or altered (PRIMARY, source 30).\[33\] This SOP's ban on AI imagery means no post should ever need a label.

### B14. Incident and Rollback Steps
14.1 **Muse proposes a prohibited action** (posting, replying, drafting text, editing an image): tap Deny. Open the Activity log (assistant icon) to confirm nothing ran (PRIMARY, source 5).\[43\] Restate the standing rule.
14.2 **Muse took an action:**
   - Undo it in Instagram yourself: delete the comment, DM or post, or revert the setting.
   - Some actions "cannot be reversed" (PRIMARY, source 5).\[42\]
   - Under the Muse Terms, you must "immediately correct" errors with affected parties (PRIMARY, source 3).\[1\]
   - Disconnect the Instagram connector (Settings → Connectors).\[39\]
   - Report it through Help & Support in Muse (PRIMARY, source 3).\[1\]
14.3 **Revoke access:** disconnect the connector in Settings → Connectors.\[39\] To fully separate, remove the Instagram account from the shared Accounts Center: Accounts Center → Accounts → Remove (PRIMARY, sources 4, 27).\[28\]
14.4 **Delete Muse data:** delete messages and side chats; edit or delete Memory.md; ask Muse to "forget [topic]." For a full wipe, use Reset Muse, which "permanently delete[s] all information including chat history, files and active tasks" (PRIMARY, sources 6–7).\[44\]\[53\]
14.5 **Instagram enforcement action:** go to Account Status → Removed content → Request a review. A reply can take up to 90 days. For a disabled account, follow the on-screen review prompts at login. "If your account was deleted, there's no way to restore it" (PRIMARY, source 29).\[31\]\[32\]
14.6 **Sentinel note:** Sentinel enforces the approval prompts outside the model (PRIMARY, sources 2, 6).\[3\]\[11\]\[53\]\[54\] It can only enforce the settings Anthony chose, which is why "Always ask" and "Deny" are mandatory.
