Minotaur Depths

Prototype: one poem, four depths

Pinch or tap
Go in a level. Pinch closed, or tap the empty ground, to go out.
Swipe sideways
Move through time: the earlier draft of the poem, or the steps of a revised line.
Drag up or down
Hand to type. Partway shows both.
Press and hold
See what is underneath: the sheet below, or where a line sits on its page.
Corner mark
Shows one box per setting type that applies at this depth. Tap a box to raise its choices; tap a choice to apply it.

Keys: Enter in, Esc out, ← → time, ↑ ↓ hand and type, H hold, Space the settings row. Only poem 1 (diary1 and its draft on diary62a) opens. The number under the poem is a placeholder until numbering is decided. Depth 4 is a sketch; single lines at the archive depth are not built yet.
