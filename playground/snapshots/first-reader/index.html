Transcription

    Marks

    • Words view: struck = cancelled in the notebook; raised small text = insertion; dotted gap = illegible; dotted underline = uncertain reading.
    • A short rule = a passage copied from a published work (not reproduced).

    Revisions

    • Step order is an editorial reconstruction from the transcription, not a record of how the page was written. Lines are assumed written top to bottom; each cancellation or insertion is placed after its stanza.
    • stanza written · new draft · revision · order uncertain (nested, stacked or partly illegible changes).
    • Across drafts lines are matched by shared words. = same · ≈ changed · + new · − dropped. Highlighted words are new since the previous draft.

    Hand ↔ Font

    • Each located line of the scan crossfades to the same words set in Etceter4 Diary, a typeface built from letters on these pages, at the line's measured position and x-height. Unlocated writing stays as ink.
