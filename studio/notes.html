    Placement and reflection timing

    Footnote: a line or word anchor, revealed only on request. Endnote: a version-associated note revealed after the poem. Reflection: a dated personal note; endnote placement is a proposal.

    Immediate reflection: record the note date when written. After a gap: add a later revision with its own note date. Neither option changes the poem’s recorded date. No delay is selected automatically.
