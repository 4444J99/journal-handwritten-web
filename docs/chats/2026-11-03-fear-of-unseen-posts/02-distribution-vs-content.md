## Asked
Whether low attention means the mechanics (tags, timing) are wrong or the content is wrong.

## Answered / produced
- New accounts get too little distribution for numbers to diagnose either.
- Three diagnostic cases once posts are seen: few saw it (distribution); many saw, few responded (work or packaging); some saw, a few responded strongly (resonance signal).
- Noted handwriting scans are a format variable separate from poem quality; test the same poem in another format before judging the poem.
- Suggested shipping a few poems on schedule, then checking which case applies.
- No files or artifacts.
