## Consequences for this packet

- The manual workflow uses available native figures and a private weekly record; it requires no paid history export, Creator conversion, scheduler or Muse subscription. If a metric is unavailable, preserve that fact.
- D02 requires actual Accounts Center membership and effective scope before any Muse trial, including manual inputs where automatic connections could already exist. Separate email alone does not establish connector separation. No account was inspected or changed.
- D11 records Instagram's applicable regional notice separately from Muse's interaction-training setting. The documented Muse setting applies to previous interactions according to Meta; that is not evidence of model unlearning or this account's present state.
- Goals are now documented, correcting the old uncertainty. They remain inactive. #59's old scheduled-agent proposal is superseded; no replacement reminder or recurring job is created.
- Exposure response is the [weekly SOP's hold-and-record procedure](weekly-sop.md), followed by the author's and T03's decision under the canonical release contract. No automated deletion, legal classification, filing or re-release rule is introduced here.
- The proposed +24h/+7d observation ages and D06 numeric options are operational choices for author review, not platform requirements. Distribution observations remain separate from judgments of poem quality.
